KGCR delivery roadmap

A phase-by-phase plan for the Knowledge Graph-Based Cloud Configuration Recommendation framework. Work is split across three students, each phase has an exit gate, dedicated testing, and stated prerequisites. Durations are indicative and should be anchored to your term calendar; the underlying plan is dependency-ordered, not date-driven.

Environment build, cross-account IAM, cost controls, CI and invariants, agents, plan/apply, CLI. Owns INFRA and SYS.

BKnowledge, ontology and data

L1 encoding, gold set, intent-first generator, labelling pipeline, defect taxonomy. Owns ONTO and DATA.

Advisor model, recommender, intent reconstruction, explainer, experiment suite and human study. Owns ML, XAI, EVAL.

IInstructor

Gold-set crosswalk review (D13), hard-gate sign-off, and the one practitioner in the human-study pilot (D17).

Start early, regardless of phase order

Five items whose lead time or risk means they cannot wait for their turn in sequence

  1. Budget alarms, day one (P0). A forgotten always-on service, not compute, is the dominant cost risk.
  2. Ontology encoding (P1). The slow, unglamorous foundation every compliance claim rests on. Start before the interesting parts.
  3. Sycophancy test harness (P6). The hard gate. Prototype it early; a failure forces an architecture change before P7 to P11 are built.
  4. Intent-reconstruction evaluation (P8). The most uncertain component, and its evaluation is free from the intent-first corpus. Prototype by week 7 to de-risk.
  5. Human-study ethics approval (P11). Long institutional lead time. File by week 6 or the study slips.

Cross-cutting tracks (run throughout)

CI and invariants

Student A. INV-1 to INV-7 as automated tests; the adversarial corpus and the invariance test run in CI from the moment each subsystem lands.

Reproducibility

Student A. Pin model and graph versions, seeds, and run records from day one so every result re-derives from (spec hash, graph version, model version).

Documentation

All. Keep the FD set and the decision register current as decisions land, so the write-up is assembled, not reconstructed.

Ethics and recruitment

Student C and instructor. File human-study ethics approval by week 6; the instructor is the practitioner reviewer for both the gold set (D13) and the study (D17).

Cost governance

Student A. Weekly resource sweep. Continuously-billing services (Config, GuardDuty, NAT) enabled only during demo windows, disabled immediately after.
